Guide

Understanding your results

PASE breaks how you naturally operate into four directions: Practical, Action, Social, and Emotional. Every question is a forced choice between the four — there's no "correct" option, just the one closest to how you'd actually respond.

01

How the score works

Across all 30 questions, we tally which type you picked each time. Your percentage for each type is just that count out of 30 — so all four always add up to 100%.

02

How your archetype is picked

We look at your ranked scores, highest to lowest. If one type clearly leads the pack, you get a pure archetype for that type. If your top two are close together and well ahead of the bottom two, you get a combo archetype built from that pair. If all four scores are close to each other, you land on The All-Rounder instead — no single lean dominates.

03

What it isn't

This is a for-fun self-typing tool inspired by the PASE framework, not a validated psychological instrument. Treat your result as a mirror to think with, not a label to live inside.
